# Experiment Brief

- **Concept:** What does the user experience with Brain?
- **Question:** What are we trying to learn?
- **Smallest slice:** What can we make usable now?
- **Flow:** How does the user enter, interact, and leave?
- **State:** What persists, who owns it, and how is it cleared?
- **Evidence:** What observable behavior would make the experiment worthwhile?
- **Demo:** How will the user try it; what is mocked?
- **Removal:** How can we retire it without disturbing other modes?

Keep this short. Leave unresolved choices labeled as questions, not decisions.
